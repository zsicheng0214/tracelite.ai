Read the scores, not just the rank

Counterfactual shift drops, exposure regimes, and multi-run reliability capture orthogonal facets of adaptation.

01

Base@1, Target@1 & Δshift

Base@1 measures single-run solve rate in the standard greenhouse container; Target@1 measures solve rate when a single verified OS property shifts. Δshift = Target@1 − Base@1 isolates adaptation loss.

02

Exposed vs. Latent regimes

Across 59 Exposed tasks, discrepancies surface as immediate sample errors; across 58 Latent tasks, sample runs pass silently while production constraints reside in cron, mounts, privileges, or quotas.

03

Pass@3 vs. Pass^3 reliability

Pass@3 requires at least one pass across three independent trials per task; strict production reliability Pass^3 requires consistent passes across every executed trial.

Evaluation scope & accounting

TwinEnv-Bench evaluates controlled micro-scale POSIX scripting, systems, and data-engineering tasks (median 22-line reference solution) across K = 3 repeats per cell (6,318 scheduled trials, 100% terminal completion). Primary rankings condition on non-refusal executed runs so provider gateway content-filter false positives are not conflated with sandbox errors, while the Refusal-as-0 selector scores all 351 target slots end-to-end.
