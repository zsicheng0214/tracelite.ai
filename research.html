Research Directions

Agent harness evaluation

Measure task outcomes and harness obligations separately, and study how observed behavior varies across model and harness configurations.

Execution traces and evidence

Connect model-visible information, native harness records, and environment observations while preserving their sources and visibility limits.

Mechanism-oriented task design

Design tasks that exercise specific mechanisms, from output recovery to tool execution and session handoffs, with explicit evidence contracts.

Failure diagnosis and attribution

Use declared mechanism chains to identify candidate failure links, keeping observed co-occurrence distinct from validated causal claims.
